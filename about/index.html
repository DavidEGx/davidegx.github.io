From match to insight

Review without rushing the decision

  1. Open a position or match Start with a fresh board, find a tournament game, or import a completed Board Game Arena match.
  2. Move through the replay Step one action or one full move at a time and rotate the board whenever another viewpoint helps.
  3. Examine the alternatives Check tile availability, possible placements, unfinished scores, and annotations before continuing.
